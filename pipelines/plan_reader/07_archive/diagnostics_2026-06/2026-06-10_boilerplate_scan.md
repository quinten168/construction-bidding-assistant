## How this feeds the disposition (Task C)

- **DROP** tier = section is ≥75% boilerplate char-volume AND holds no protected gotcha — safe to skip at ingest (signature/forms/standard-condition prose).
- **DOWN_WEIGHT** = ≥50% boilerplate but mixed with real content or protected gotchas — keep in the store, de-prioritize at retrieval.
- **KEEP** = mostly project-specific — the bid schedule, real special provisions, gotchas.
- These suggestions are written to `data/section_disposition.csv` (the editable toss-out taxonomy); ingest reads that, not this report.
